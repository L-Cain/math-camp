Simultaneity Bias


Supply:
$$Q=\alpha_1*P+\alpha_2 z_1+\delta_t$$
Demand:
$$Q=\beta_1*P+\beta_2 z_2+\epsilon_t$$

Two equations two unknowns:

So:


$$0=\alpha_1*P_t+\alpha_2 z_1+\delta_t-(\beta_1*P_t+\beta_2 z_2+\epsilon_t)$$

$$-\alpha_1*P_t+\beta_1*P_t=\alpha_2 z_1+\delta_t-(\beta_2 z_2+\epsilon_t)$$

$$P_t(-\alpha_1+\beta_1)=\alpha_2 z_1+\delta_t-(\beta_2 z_2+\epsilon_t)$$


$$P_t= \frac{\alpha_2 z_1+\delta_t-(\beta_2 z_2+\epsilon_t)}{\beta_1-\alpha_1}$$




$$P_t= \frac{\alpha_2}{{\beta_1-\alpha_1}} z_1-\frac{\beta_2}{\beta_1-\alpha_1}z_2 +\frac{(\delta_t-\epsilon_t)}{\beta_1-\alpha_1}$$



$$Q_t=\alpha_1* \left(\frac{\alpha_2}{{\beta_1-\alpha_1}} z_1-\frac{\beta_2}{\beta_1-\alpha_1}z_2 +\frac{\delta_t-\epsilon_t}{\beta_1-\alpha_1}\right)+\alpha_2 z_1+\delta_t$$

$$Q_t=\alpha_2 (1+\frac{\alpha_1}{{\beta_1-\alpha_1}}) z_1-\frac{\alpha_1\beta_2}{\beta_1-\alpha_1}z_2 +\frac{\alpha_1*(\delta_t-\epsilon_t)+(\beta_1-\alpha_1)\delta_t}{\beta_1-\alpha_1}$$


$$Q_t=\alpha_2 (1+\frac{\alpha_1}{{\beta_1-\alpha_1}}) z_1-\frac{\alpha_1\beta_2}{\beta_1-\alpha_1}z_2 +\frac{\beta_1\delta_t}{\beta_1-\alpha_1}-\frac{\alpha_1\epsilon}{\beta_1-\alpha_1}$$
