\small

\begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|} \hline
{\bf Symbol}&{\bf How to read it}&{\bf Notes on meaning and usage}\\ \hline 
$a=b$&$a$ equals $b$& $a$ and $b$ have exactly the same value \\ \hline 

$A \equiv B$&Equal by definition &\$ 1 $\equiv$ 100 pennies \\ \hline

$P \Rightarrow Q$&  $P$ implies $Q$ &If $P$ true, then $Q$ true; Don't confuse with $\rightarrow$ \\ \hline

$f: X\rightarrow Y$& Function $f$ maps set $X$ into set $Y$ & Every function is a mapping \\ \hline

$P\Leftrightarrow Q$&$P$ equivalent to $Q$, or & $P$ and $Q$ imply each other\\ 

$P$ iff $Q$& $P$ if and only if $Q$& \\ \hline

$(a, b)$&Open interval from $a$ to $b$ &Points between $a$ and $b$, not including $a$ and $b$ \\ \hline

$[a, b]$&Closed interval from $a$ to $b$  & Points between $a$ and $b$, including $a$ and $b$\\ \hline

$\mathbb{R}$&The real numbers & {\it All} numbers either positive, negative or zero \\ \hline

$\mathbb{Z}$&The integers & $..., -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, ...$\\ \hline

$\mathbb{N}$&The natural numbers & $ 1, 2, 3, 4, ...$\\ \hline

$\mathbb{R}^n$&$n$-dimensional vector space over $\mathbb{R}$ &Ex: $(2, 4.1, 0.2, 1, 3)\in \mathbb{R}^5$ \\ \hline

$a\in B$& $a$ is an element of $B$ &Not to confuse with $\subset$, which refers to set inclusion \\

&& or $\epsilon$, which is the Greek letter epsilon\\ \hline

$a\not\in B$& $a$ is not an element of $B$ & \\ \hline

$A\subset B$&Set $A$ is a strict subset of $B$ &Anything in $A$ is also in $B$ and $A\not= B$ \\ \hline

$A\subseteq B$&Set $A$ is a weak subset of $B$ &Anything in $A$ is also in $B$, and possibly $A=B$ \\ \hline

$A \cup B$&$A$ union $B$ & Set of all points that are either in $A$ or $B$ or both \\ \hline

$A\cap B$&$A$ intersection $B$ &Set of all points that are both in $A$ and $B$ \\ \hline

$\exists$&There exists &Just shorthand \\ \hline

s.t.&Such that &Shorthand.  Sometimes also written as $:$ \\ \hline

$\forall x$& For all $x$ &Ex: $\forall x>0$, $\exists y\in \mathbb{R}$ s.t. $y<x$ and $y>0$ \\ \hline

$\exists !$&There exists a unique &$\exists !$ $x: x=4.78943$ \\ \hline

$\emptyset$&The empty set &The set containing nothing (scary...) \\ \hline

$2^X$&Set of all subsets of $X$,  &$X=\{a, b, c\}\Rightarrow$ \\ 

&also called the power set of $X$&$2^X=\{\emptyset, \{a\}, \{b\}, \{c\}, \{a,b\}, \{a, c\}, \{b, c\}, \{a, b, c\}\}$\\ \hline

$\{ \cdot \cdot \cdot \}$& An unordered set of things & Ex: $\{a, b, c\}=\{c, a, b\}=\{b, c, a\}$, etc.\\ \hline

$(\cdot \cdot \cdot)$& A vector, or ordered set &Ex: $(a, b, c)$ as a point in 3-D space \\ \hline

$X\setminus Y$&$X$ minus all elements of  $Y$  &Ex: $2^{\{a, b, c\}}\setminus 2^{\{a, b\}}= \{\{c\}, \{a, c\}, \{b, c\}, \{a, b, c\}\}$ \\ \hline

$|X|$&Cardinality of $X$ (\# elements in $X$) & $|\{a, b, c, d\}|=4$\\ \hline



\end{tabular}
